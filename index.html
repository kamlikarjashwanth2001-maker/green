📜 India's Ethanol Policy Timeline

Key milestones in India's green fuel journey

2003

🚀 EBP Programme Launched

India launched the Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP) programme with 5% blending target in select states.

2008

⛽ E5 Mandate

Government mandated 5% ethanol blending in petrol across 20 states and 4 Union Territories.

2013

📈 E10 Target Set

National Policy on Biofuels set 10% ethanol blending target. Vehicle manufacturers started making E10-ready vehicles.

2018

🏭 National Biofuel Policy

New National Policy on Biofuels approved. Allowed use of damaged food grains for ethanol production. E20 target set for 2030.

2021

🎯 E20 Target Advancement

PM Modi advanced E20 target from 2030 to 2025. E10 achieved in many states. Flex-fuel vehicle development started.

2023

🌿 E20 Rollout Begins

E20 fuel launched at select petrol pumps. All new vehicles mandated to be E20 compatible. India achieved 12% average blending.

2025

🏆 E20 Nationwide (Target)

Goal: 20% ethanol blending across all fuel stations in India. Expected to save ₹30,000 crore annually in oil imports.

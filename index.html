ℹ️ What is Ethanol Blended Fuel?

The Indian Government under the Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP) Programme is promoting ethanol blending to reduce pollution and oil imports.

E5 - 5% Ethanol E10 - 10% Ethanol E20 - 20% Ethanol E85 - 85% Ethanol
  • India currently sells E10 fuel at most petrol pumps.
  • E20 fuel is being rolled out from April 2023.
  • Vehicles manufactured after 2008 generally support up to E10.
  • Vehicles manufactured after April 2023 are E20 compliant by mandate.
  • Older vehicles (before 2008) may face issues with ethanol blends.

⚠️ Disclaimer: This tool provides general guidance only. Always consult your vehicle manufacturer's manual for official compatibility information.
